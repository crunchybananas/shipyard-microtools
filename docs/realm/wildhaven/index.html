Village journal

    ⌂

    A village, at your pace.

    Put people to work, turn raw materials into useful goods, and decide what your town will become. The restored bell is the beginning: research new crafts, choose a charter and trade beyond the island.

    Build
    Choose a building and an open space. On touch screens, tap a site to preview, then choose Build here. Materials are reserved immediately; your builders work through the project queue. Reorder, pause or cancel projects in the town book. Press R to turn a plan.
    Make a living
    Workshops need workers and materials. Assign people in the town book. A mill turns grain into flour, then a bakery turns flour into food. The founders keep gathering a little to help you recover.
    Trade
    Once six people live here, trade surplus food at the landing. A supply skiff every 90 seconds at normal speed can bring timber or stone while keeping supper in reserve.
    Welcome people
    Finished homes add beds. Food, morale and services influence arrivals. Upgrade buildings to improve their capacity and output; the work takes builders and temporarily stops production.
    The coast
    A town of twenty draws unfamiliar sails after the bell is restored. The watch shows demands, deadlines, and exact cargo at risk. Pay to end a warning, shelter goods, or give supplied guards time to prepare a patrol. People and buildings survive a failed defense.
    The frontier
    Press F or choose Frontier. Recruit named citizens into the company and send them along the island trails to discover neighbors. Envoys, trade and alliances offer peaceful paths; declaring war creates real attacks. Walls and towers take materials and a resident’s labor. Leave gates in your routes home. Claims open new land after an outpost is finished.
    Command
    Select troops in Company, choose Move or Attack, then tap a target on the island. Hold keeps them near their position; Retreat sends them home. The panel folds away while you aim. Escape cancels the command. Tend wounded residents and watch the battle log.
    Fieldbook
    Press B to find four old places. Send a named surveyor to read their stories, then choose restoration or salvage. A restorer walks to the site and returns. Recall keeps paid materials and progress; nearby enemies send fieldworkers home. Restored places improve specific village jobs.
    Sound
    Enable sound with the music button. Work and footsteps follow nearby people; distant activity fades. Sound settings adjusts effects and the island ambience separately.
    Explore
    Drag to move. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Q / E or right-drag to turn. Arrow keys also move the view. Home recenters.
    Take your time
    A calendar day lasts six minutes at 1×. Jobs, combat, meals and arrivals keep their usual pace. Rates and countdowns use minutes at 1×; 3× accelerates both play and the calendar. Space pauses. 1–8 choose from the open building category. T opens the town book. Escape cancels. Ctrl / ⌘ Z cancels the latest unfinished construction for unused materials. Tap a building to inspect or salvage it.

    Your town saves on this device. Projects, jobs, research and frontier orders survive your return. The simulation pauses while you are away.
