      How to play

      Start with homes, food and building materials. Assign workers in Town → People. Later, add workshops, research new buildings and trade with your neighbors.

      Build
      Choose a building and an open space. On touch screens, tap a site to preview, then choose Build here. Materials are reserved immediately; your builders work through the project queue. Reorder, pause or cancel projects in the town book. Press R to turn a plan.
      Production
      Workshops need workers and materials. Assign people in the town book. A mill turns grain into flour, then a bakery turns flour into food. The founders keep gathering a little to help you recover.
      Research
      Plan a project in Town → Research. Allocate 0%, 25%, 50% or 100% of new research supplies to its three stages. Each stage stops collecting when funded. Staffed, supplied scholars do the work. Fund now uses existing stores if you want to speed up a stage. Partial deliveries stay reserved. Pause a project or stop it to return the current stage’s supplies; completed experiments are spent.
      Stone and ore
      Stoneworks and mines share up to three deposits within five tiles. Reserves run out and rebuilding does not refill them. Surveying research and a level-two upgrade open finite deeper seams. Inspect a workplace to see what remains.
      Farm animals
      Animal husbandry unlocks goat pastures and henhouses. Buy animals in their shelter, assign keepers and provide grain. Milk and eggs enter the food store. Two adults can breed while there is room; kids mature after two calendar days of care and chicks after one. Breeding can be paused. Upgrades keep the herd and add space.
      Trade
      Once six people live here, trade surplus food at the landing. A supply skiff every 90 seconds at normal speed can bring timber or stone while keeping food in reserve.
      Welcome people
      Finished homes add beds. Food, morale and services influence arrivals. Upgrade buildings to improve their capacity and output; the work takes builders and temporarily stops production.
      The coast
      After the bell is restored and the town reaches twenty residents, repeated exports can attract coastal cargo demands. Shared patrols with neighbors reduce this risk. The watch shows demands, deadlines, and exact cargo at risk. Pay to end a warning, shelter goods, or give supplied guards time to prepare a patrol. People and buildings survive a failed coastal defense. Barracks staff prepare this cargo patrol; recruited Company soldiers protect against attackers on the island. Recruiting a watch worker removes them from their old job.
      Exploration and neighbors
      Press F or choose Explore. Send residents to scout regions, gather supplies or deliver neighbor requests. Reports and goods arrive when they return home. Envoys establish contact with nearby towns; shared projects unlock crops and coastal patrols. Neighbor armies attack only after you declare war. To build outside your starting region, finish an outpost there.
      Gardens
      Choose carrots, beans or herbs in Explore → Gardens. A new crop starts after the current harvest. Crops need an assigned gardener and an unpaused garden. These harvests go into gathering stores for neighbor projects; gardens also produce food for residents.
      Command
      In Explore → Company, choose Ready company or recall a saved squad. Defend Town guards the area around the barracks and rests troops inside when it is clear. Return to barracks brings troops home for recovery. Hold Here keeps troops at their current position. Move and Attack let you tap a destination or an existing hostile target. Group select lets you tap soldiers to toggle them, then Done; Cancel restores your previous selection. You can also use the large roster buttons, or Shift-click on desktop. Orders, squads and wounded residents survive saving. Keep routes to barracks open and watch the battle log. Recruit a field medic and select them with your soldiers before Move or Attack. Medics carry six dressings, treat nearby injuries after a short break from damage, and refill at barracks using food and cloth. Severe wounds still require rest.
      Walls
      Open Build → Defenses (also in Explore). Under Stone wall, choose Draw a wall line, then tap its start and end on the island. Review the full cost and choose Build sections. Place a gate in a gap and keep it open for passage. Residents leave their usual work to build defenses.
      Fieldbook
      Press B to find six old places. Send a named surveyor to read their stories, then choose restoration or salvage. A restorer walks to the site and returns. Recall keeps paid materials and progress; nearby enemies send fieldworkers home. Restored places improve specific village jobs.
      Sound
      Enable sound with the music button. Work and footsteps follow nearby people; distant activity fades. Sound settings adjusts effects and the island ambience separately.
      Camera
      Drag to move. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Q / E or right-drag to turn. Arrow keys also move the view. Home recenters.
      Stores & seasons
      Tap a resource to see what makes it, what uses it, and useful next steps. Tap the date for the seasonal calendar. Each season lasts three in-game days; time pauses while you are away. In autumn, a harvest festival can turn surplus food into a day of extra morale. Its preview shows the cost and leaves two town meals. Winter changes the scenery without reducing crops or production.
      Take your time
      A calendar day lasts six minutes at 1×. Jobs, combat, meals and arrivals keep their usual pace. Rates and countdowns use minutes at 1×; 3× accelerates both play and the calendar. Space pauses. 1–8 choose from the open building category. T opens the town book. Escape cancels. Ctrl / ⌘ Z cancels the latest unfinished construction for unused materials. Tap a building to inspect or salvage it.

      Open More → Your towns to create, rename or duplicate a town. Each town saves separately on this browser; creating a new one keeps your existing progress. Download backups from Your towns. Projects, jobs, research and frontier orders survive your return. The simulation pauses while you are away.
