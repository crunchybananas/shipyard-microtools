# Wildhaven: a direction with a first payoff

The reviewed Wildhaven release is live through [PR #152](https://github.com/crunchybananas/shipyard-microtools/pull/152), merged as `5e15c48`. [Deployment 37566857248](https://github.com/crunchybananas/shipyard-microtools/actions/runs/37566857248) succeeded. A disposable touch browser loaded the live game, verified seven key module/stylesheet hashes against the merged source, opened all 15 research nodes and 19 prerequisite links, and followed tools storage to its import quote without page or loading errors. This publication supersedes the earlier release review’s pre-publication status.

The follow-up on `codex/wildhaven-first-bread` addresses one specific gap: choosing a direction did not carry the player from research to a useful working economy. It adds an optional **first-bread goal** on the Food town card. Selecting it costs nothing, closes Council and reveals the next action in the existing objective card. It grants no charter benefits; formal policy rules and switch costs remain unchanged.

The guide follows the real town through school, Timber framing, sawmill, Fieldcraft, grain farm, Flour and fire, windmill and bakery. It distinguishes missing materials, paused time, construction, supplies, requested workers and actually assigned workers. Active research is respected even when unrelated. Learned discoveries link directly to their building plans; the research graph marks the three bread discoveries while retaining the rest of the graph. The charter description no longer implies a town hall must be built before choosing a policy.

A first-bread record is written only when an authoritative bakery tick actually increases food stock. Forecasts, imports, inspection, full storage and unstaffed buildings cannot award it. The optional goal and production proof survive saves and remain independent between companion towns. Older saves default safely; an existing bakery can establish proof on its next real production tick. No balancing values, artwork or other game paths changed.

First bread arrived on Day 14 after **265.3 wall-clock seconds** of play. Reload preserved the milestone and journal; returning to town goals retained the earned proof.
- That full journey preceded the final counter, route-label contrast and redundant-path-prompt polish. Both runs have source provenance.

Its checkpoint used for the staffing test is explicitly staged through the supported priority command; it is not presented as browser-earned history.
