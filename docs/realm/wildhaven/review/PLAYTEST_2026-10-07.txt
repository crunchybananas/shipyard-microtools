Computer-control playtest — October 7, 2026

Build: public GitHub Pages game after PR 152, before the compact-calendar change.
Surface: Codex in-app browser, approximately 665 x 675 pixels. This is a compact desktop pane, not a phone/device test. Local preview access was rejected by the browser URL policy; the published HTTPS game was accessible. No local-save interaction was performed.
Method: visible controls and world clicks only. No injected resources, state writes, review clock, or simulation shortcuts. A new public-origin village was started because the opening screen had no Continue option. Only 1x and Pause were used.

Observed sequence
- Started with six residents on Day 1, chose a cottage and paused to understand placement.
- Clicking the exposed strip just above the catalogue did nothing. An unobscured meadow click successfully queued the cottage.
- Resumed at 1x, expanded the ambition, enabled the sound option, and queued an orchard.
- Cottage finished while reading the workforce panel; the two builders moved to the orchard.
- Used Ada's Find control. By then she was farming; her inspector correctly said Picking fruit and explained experience.
- Queued a woodcutter near trees. Its entrance warning was useful; rotating once allowed construction.
- Queued stoneworks and opened the fieldbook. Day 2 brought two residents while projects continued.
- Sent the newly arrived Hollis to survey the waystone for six food. The panel showed travel, then 8 / 22 seconds of study before I paused.

Findings to compare with testers
1. HIGH: the catalogue and placement card covered most of this pane. A large persistent header, management row, categories, build cards and lower links competed with the actual place being built.
2. HIGH: reading management/fieldbook screens leaves time running. The village advances while its activity is covered. This supports investigating the felt pace, but I did not reproduce five days in thirty seconds or reach an invasion.
3. MEDIUM: successful placement immediately showed There is already a building or construction site here under a success toast. It looked like an error despite spending supplies and creating the project.
4. MEDIUM: Find brought Ada closer, but her inspector hid the resident I was trying to watch in the narrow pane.
5. POSITIVE: cottage construction and automatic job reassignment felt responsive. Keep those timings while slowing the calendar.
6. POSITIVE: the rotation hint explained a blocked entrance and resolved it without guessing a different building.
7. POSITIVE: the fieldbook clearly quoted provisions, travel, study and return, and identified the actual resident and current activity.
8. NOT ASSESSED: perceived sound quality/balance. The interface confirmed sound enabled, but this control session does not provide a reliable listening signal. Testers should listen for repetition and balance.

Suggested tester prompts
- What were you trying to do when time felt too fast? Note the speed setting and open panel.
- Can you watch the selected worker or construction site while its controls are open?
- After a battle, is the problem the day number, another threat arriving, or too little recovery time? These are different timers.
- Can you find Build, staffing, Pause, the fieldbook and the way back home without instruction?
- What sound becomes repetitive or too loud after ten minutes?

Changes prepared from these observations
- Six-minute calendar at 1x; movement, work, combat and economic intervals preserved.
- Existing saves retain their current day on upgrade.
- Build/Town dock on all input types, catalogue closed during placement, no HUD wordmark.
- Painted transparent resource icons from image generation; actual 3D model thumbnails retained.
- Successful placement explains Queued instead of presenting an occupied-site error.
