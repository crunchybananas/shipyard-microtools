# Wildhaven

A fresh 3D settlement game, built alongside Realm. A working miniature island: homes, orchards, timber yards and a restored village bell. The pleasure is watching a place become inhabited through your choices.

## Visual direction

Sea glass #438f94, deep tide #285a62, clover #89a960, butter plaster #f2d4a0, kiln red #bf634a, ink #233c37. Soft afternoon shadows, actual geometry, readable sculpted villagers. An authored crescent shore and wooded upland give the settlement a place to grow into. No fog of war, screen particles, SVG effects or borrowed sprite atlases.

The world fills the window. A small village sign anchors the upper left; supplies live in a compact top strip. The current ambition is written in a small left-hand notebook. Building choices sit on a single bottom shelf, illustrated by the actual 3D assets. A contextual inspector appears only when useful. Rounded humanist system typography and Georgia for the village name keep interface quiet.

## Gameplay

Build a cottage, secure food, establish timber and stone production, and bring people home. Siting buildings near natural resources increases production. Food and housing govern arrivals. Restore the old bell when the village is established; the arrival of the ferry is the first ending, with continued building afterward. Placement previews explain constraints before spending. Undo and salvage make experiments forgiving.

## Technology

One existing dependency: vendored Three.js, needed for a real 3D renderer and glTF models. Plain browser modules, HTML, CSS, Web Audio and localStorage. No framework, bundler, physics engine, UI library, external CDN or account. Original Blender source accompanies the exported models.
