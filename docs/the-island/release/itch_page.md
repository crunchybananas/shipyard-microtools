# THE ISLAND — abyme

**A lighthouse. A model of the island it stands on. A tide that only rises.**

You wash up on an island with a light still turning and no one keeping it. In the
study, on the chart table, there is a model of the island — exact, patient, and
wrong in ways you will learn to read. The sea has been rising for years. Everything
the keeper could not say out loud, he wrote small: in a public log for the
inspector, in a private one for himself, in lampblack too fine to read without his
glass, on wax, on stone, on the backs of unsent letters.

Go down. The island repeats — the same shore, the same sky, drowned a little
further each time. It isn't deeper water. It's earlier time.

- **A non-linear written world** — sixteen readable hands-worth of letters, logs,
  ledgers, and marks, three optional story chains, and a codex that assembles what
  you find into the shape of a life.
- **His rounds** — the keeper's daily acts, findable and performable.
- **The inspection** — file the record of a life for the office that asked, or
  carry it down and leave it with him. Neither is scored. Both are remembered.
- **A shore that shrinks** — what you lose stays lost, and the endings read back
  what you kept.
- **Made of code** — every mesh, texture and note synthesized or hand-built;
  three.js and one island. Plays in the browser, mouse or touch. ~30–90 minutes
  the first time; the deep reads take longer.

*headphones recommended · a world made of code, and a voice rising from the bottom*
