# A way through The Island

The light above the beach is on. There is a path up to it.

Before working at the model, look through the other door. Light the small lamp
beside the bed. The room is yours to return to. The spare chair has a note tucked
underneath it.

In the study, try the little valve. Follow the exposed road, find what the chest
holds, and try laying it over the crack in the model. The crank beside the model
moves the hour. Once you have tried those things, the brass floor plate can take
you down. Stand in its centre and touch it twice.

Below, the full-sized valve behaves differently. Stay to see what happens around
it. Someone in the kelp will answer if you give them time. The boatwright's slate
records an earlier attempt. Then the plate will bring you back to the room.

For the next crossing, you will need to listen more closely. The music box and
the dawn bird sing nearly the same tune. The stone arc can answer. What it opens
belongs in the model lighthouse.

There is a drain below the standing stones. Its ramp leads to a ledger left out of
the rain. Back at the lighthouse, the fitted lens releases the stair rope. Take
your time going up. The windows show how far you have climbed; the watch book
on the gallery records someone who had to make the trip rather often.

The light will show an order on the cliff after dark. The books on the shelf name
the instruments behind those figures. The readings are on the instruments, not
in the books. Later in the afternoon, the stones' joined shadow points to a door.
Bring the line from below that door back to the study.

Under the hatch, look through both doorways before returning. East, a lighthouse
hangs over dark water. West, two shallow steps lead into another study. Someone
has put the wet papers in a bread tin. The lid still lifts.

The next descent passes through the familiar shallows to the old hall. Spend some
time with the register. The visitor on the shore also needs a little patience.
Read old papers again; some have later writing on their backs.

At the source, someone has left an unfinished boat and a page about it. The small
figure at the model is waiting. Let your hands rest while you look. Then the brass
index can select what happens between the rooms.

Come back by the plate. There is a small boat in the east room that can finally be
tried in the model sea. The last decision is at the refuge lamp: one touch to
consider it, another to commit. You can step away before the second touch.

`J` opens your notes. If you want more direct help, **Trace a lead** offers it
without changing what you have found.
