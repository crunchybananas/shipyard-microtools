# THE STRATA — one island, several receivers

The drowned worlds are physical strata in a shared stack. They may carry traces
from different years, but they are not flashbacks and the player is not reliving
another person’s identity. A descent changes scale and point of view: work that
felt small above arrives below as water, pressure, weather, and inherited repair.

| Level | id | Felt world | What the player must learn |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | surface | a beautiful instrument and a dry refuge | control is useful, pleasurable, and incomplete from one side |
| L2 | shallows | familiar ground receiving new water | an upper action becomes somebody else’s environment |
| L3 | inspection | a drowned hall and an incomplete account | a system can count work while omitting its receiver |
| L4 | source | mechanisms at their limit and another hand | regard precedes any honest choice about a boundary |

## The material rule

Every level is the same island rendered from the same current world state. The
differences are evidence, not dream logic:

- water displaced above is received below;
- objects lost from one shore can appear as material on another;
- measurements and records disagree because they were made from different sides;
- current and earlier hands remain distinct;
- ascent does not erase what a descent revealed.

Artifacts can be older or newer than the place where water uncovers them. Their
dates establish provenance only. They must never announce a secret identity,
translate a stratum into a life chapter, or explain the game’s theme for the
player.

## The encounter rule

Each required presence asks for an embodied capacity rather than a lore answer.

| Presence | Capacity | Observable proof |
|---|---|---|
| Tide-Figure | stop pursuing | it holds together and returns one low note |
| Watcher | sustain attention | it can no longer close distance unseen |
| Lower Hand | remain near, still, and looking | it stays other while the mechanism becomes readable |

The Keeper may ask a small number of sensory questions. No presence tells the
player who they are or supplies a moral ranking of the four final operations.

## The return rule

The dry east room is the route’s boundary, not optional backstory. The player
first makes a light there and later returns with a disposition chosen below. The
same shelter can remain imperfect, open, and bounded. The ending reports what the
operation did to both sides; it does not certify virtue, cure the world, or reveal
that two characters were secretly one.
