# THE ERAS — the strata are not depths (AAA-A1, #129)

Canon since 2026-08-06 (AAA_PLAN.md Part II; SPINE.md points here). The drowned
strata are ERAS of the keeper's tenancy. Diving is remembering. The island does not
drown all at once; it drowns in order, and the order is his.

| Level | id | era.key | Name | The tenancy's time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | surface | `lastday` | the last day | the tenancy's final state, as found |
| L2 | shallows | `arrival` | the arrival years | first descents, small lies, wonder |
| L3 | midwater | `inspection` | the inspection years | the world measures him |
| L4 | source | `lastwinter` | the last winter | the end known, the errand set |

**The two-histories rule.** Each era also owns what the water UNCOVERED of the
deeper past during it: the founders' stone bares at L2 (the arrival years are when
he first read it), the congregation's capitals rise at L3 (the institution's
ancestor surfaces in the institution's era). Era = the keeper's time + the island's
returned past, interleaved.

**Stated once.** The game says the reframe out loud exactly once — 8 seconds after
first splashdown at L2 (`once('eraThreshold')`, puzzles tick): whisper
`T.everything_down_here_is`, journal `T.the_water_is_higher` (sketch matcher
"it drowns in order"). Never repeated, never explained again. Everything after is
shown, not told (A2 events, A3 rounds, B2 color, B3 arrangements).
