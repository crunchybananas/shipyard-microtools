# ABYME — Narrative Spine

## Story sentence

A stranger learns to command a beautiful model, descends into the lives affected by
that command, and returns to decide what responsibility should cross the boundary.

## Central dramatic law

> Every convenience has a receiver.

On the surface, scale feels like wonder and agency: a small gesture moves a sea,
raises a bridge, turns a sky, and lights a tower. Below, the same gesture is weather,
pressure, and inherited work. The descent does not punish curiosity; it gives every
action a point of view from the other side.

The persistent stack makes that law literal. Acts become water and material traces
for lower hands. Helpful shore writing travels by the same route without adding
cost.

## Emotional movement

The arc is abstract and environmental:

```text
wonder and shelter
→ agency
→ displacement
→ survival inside another hand's decision
→ measurement and accountability
→ regard
→ boundary choice
→ return to an imperfect, living surface
```

No personal history is imported whole. The island carries the emotional geometry:
safety can become enclosure, power can feel seductive, measurement can conceal a
receiver, and repair can mean tending, reversing, joining, or setting a boundary.

## The player and the other presences

The player arrives as a stranger. Their prior life is deliberately unassigned.

The Keeper voice, Tide-Figure, Watcher, Lower Hand, past workers, and current player
remain distinct presences. The story does not collapse them into one secret person.
Recognition comes from shared conditions and consequences, not sameness.

The Lower Hand is therefore not a collectible, missing half, or final boss. The
bottom asks the player to maintain proximity, gaze, and stillness long enough to
regard someone living with what arrived from above.

## Three acts

### Act I — The attractive instrument

The surface teaches an intoxicating grammar: change the model and the world obeys.

- Valve: water yields.
- Crank: time yields.
- Ruler: distance yields.
- Lens and beam: darkness yields.
- Plumb and plate: scale yields.

The signal lock makes observation collaborative across objects. The beam provides
selection and order; eight manuals route figures to working instruments; the
instruments provide counts learned through play. No authoritative page has the answer.

The act ends when the player completes the circuit and willingly touches the plate
twice.

### Act II — The receiver

The same island returns as successive strata, each with a rule rather than a lore
dump.

| Stratum | Felt world | Required encounter | Dramatic knowledge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shallows | living green water across familiar paths | witness the Upstream Hand; become still with the Tide-Figure | an act above arrives here as environment |
| Inspection | steel light, breached hall, counted work | let the register settle; hold the Watcher in view | systems can count actions while omitting receivers |
| Source | cold, spare, instruments at their limit | regard the Lower Hand; set the brass index | responsibility begins with another presence staying other |

Every gate requires embodied evidence from that stratum. The player cannot descend
by carrying only surface solutions or a debug-shaped final flag.

The Keeper speaks rarely and asks about conditions: where the player stood, what the
water carried, where it went. The voice does not announce a cosmology or assign the
player a role.

### Act III — Relation and return

After regard, a four-position index appears:

- **tend** — remain with the transfer as it stands;
- **carry** — take this hand's interventions back;
- **open** — permit cost and water to move both ways;
- **close** — hold a boundary that prevents further transfer.

The player chooses at the bottom but cannot finalize there. They must carry the
intention through the full ascent and touch the original surface plate twice. The
return matters because responsibility is not complete as an insight in the deepest
room; it must be brought back to the place where agency began.

The bell and oar remain touchable after their earlier symbolic promise. Neither
offers an escape around the return.

## Final image

All dispositions end in the same visual dignity: golden hour, the turning lamp, the
island still weathered and inhabited, a long shadow joining instrument and shore.

The world does not become pristine. The ending shows the selected operation truthfully:

- held transfer;
- reversed interventions and changed water;
- joined basins seeking a shared level;
- sealed plate with pressure held below.

The coda reports facts. It does not announce virtue, failure, healing, or a canonical
answer.

## Evidence language

Field Notes are the player's instrument of continuity.

- They record only touched, heard, read, or changed facts.
- Stable IDs carry progression; prose can improve without changing save meaning.
- Measurements, omissions, revised forms, wear, and spatial comparisons do the work.
- Later pages are earned separately when reached.
- Several hands may leave contradictory or incomplete accounts.
- Sketches identify material relationships, not abstract objectives.

**Trace a lead** is a separate consent boundary. It offers progressively clearer
relationships only when requested and never becomes evidence.

## Readable-surface standard

A readable surface should contain something its maker plausibly needed to record:
water height, maintenance, a failed trial, an unsigned return, a room kept dry, a
signal pairing, a missing authorization.

Prefer:

- concrete nouns;
- observed change;
- dates or measures when they create pressure;
- blank ruled space;
- corrections and wear;
- two facts the player can compare.

Avoid:

- speeches that summarize the theme;
- hidden sentences used as lock combinations;
- a document that selects and orders the complete hatch answer;
- a twist that reassigns who the player has been;
- moral labels attached to dispositions;
- instructions disguised as memoir.

## Recurring images

- **Model and world:** power viewed from opposite scales.
- **Waterline:** cost made spatial.
- **Lamp:** ongoing attention, not victory.
- **Hands and marks:** work has provenance.
- **Register:** official count versus lived consequence.
- **Two-touch plate:** intention followed by commitment.
- **Open door / dry room:** shelter can coexist with boundaries.
- **Signal index:** knowledge distributed across places rather than delivered by an
  authority.

## Questions the game answers

- What is the model for? To expose the distance between an action and its receiver.
- Why descend? To stand inside consequences that are invisible from the control room.
- Why return? Because understanding below has no force until it changes a relation
  above.
- What waits at the bottom? Another hand, a physical account, and a choice of boundary.
- What is the ending? The selected relation applied to the persistent world.

## Questions the game leaves open

- Who built the first model?
- How many hands have crossed the stack?
- Whether a given record is care, duty, fear, or all three.
- Which disposition the next hand would want.
- Whether any boundary can stay permanent in moving water.

Ambiguity belongs in motive and history. Core causality, interaction, and ending
authority must remain legible.

## Acceptance test

The spine is present only if a player can experience it without explanation outside
the game:

1. delight in changing the surface;
2. derive the hatch setting by carrying one signal across several physical objects;
3. encounter a lower world made wetter by work above;
4. satisfy each threshold through local observation;
5. regard the Lower Hand without merging or collecting it;
6. choose a disposition physically;
7. climb all the way back;
8. commit at the same plate where agency began;
9. see a factual, unranked consequence.

If a new feature does not strengthen one of those beats, it is atmosphere or
maintenance. It may still be valuable, but it is not part of the spine.
