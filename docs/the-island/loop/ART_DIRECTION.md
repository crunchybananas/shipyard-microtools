# Landfall — spatial and visual direction

The island should be recognisable from an uncaptioned view: a worked lighthouse
on the western headland, the low road to the stones, and a split bluff with rooms
underneath. Its shape is editable. A familiar silhouette is useful only when it
serves the journey.

## Build places around an action

The tower is experienced a tread at a time. Windows interrupt the climb with real
views of the coast. The last landing releases the view in every direction. The
watch book makes the effort part of somebody else's ordinary day.

The underground sequence contracts before it opens: hatch, narrow stair, cellar,
then the impossible hanging tower. The western doorway leads to a smaller room
with papers spread out to dry. Its tin is a physical object; opening it leaves a
change the player can come back to.

The drain is another entrance with its own height, waterline and exit. A capture
taken on the ground above it is not evidence of an interior. Room review must
include the approach, threshold, occupied space and return.

## Material and light

Use chalked masonry, dark mineral breaks, oxidised metal, worn wood and blue cloth.
Keep distant shapes calm enough to read in the miniature. Concentrate geometry
where a hand or camera comes close: treads, sill depth, rails, furniture and lids.
The distant lighthouse and pines have explicit lighter meshes.

The sea and windows supply orientation. Underground rooms need real openings and
shadow, with enough local light to read a route. Surface terrain must not render
across a room that is physically beneath it. A dark frame still needs a readable
edge, distance and reason to look.

## People and prose

The keeper writes working notes. The boatwright notices the person doing the work.
The visitor gradually carries more and records less. Allow dry jokes, unfinished
sentences and small disagreements. Metaphor belongs in actions and objects. No
biographical source, recognisable chronology or explanation of the metaphor belongs
in the game or its review folder.

## The next visual work

The original canopy silhouettes, generic study walls and some broad rock relief
still need more authored variation. The new assets are a step toward a coherent
environment, not evidence that the whole game has reached a AAA production standard.
Judge further changes from player height in daylight, dusk, rain and the deeper
strata, then through a full route. Keep the existing render budget measurable.

## Review discipline

The readable sequence groups consecutive artifact pages and folds repeated views.
All source images, actions and saved states remain available. New rooms and new
consequences get distinct frames. The manuscript is also readable as text, so
editorial decisions never depend on zooming into a screenshot.
