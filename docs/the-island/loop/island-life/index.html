01 / observation

A bird should be worth watching.

Original Blender gulls, crows and a songbird now have articulated heads, bills, wings, tails and feet. Small looks, blinks, pecks and pauses happen independently. Flight alternates between gliding and short wingbeats; shore birds return and settle after you move away.

02 / construction

The work belongs together.

The hull has ribs, overlapping strakes, fitted floorboards, seats and a keel. The cleat sits on the gunwale. Nearby shingle now fades into sand instead of breaking into hard-edged patches.

03 / contact and scale

Small errors break a place.

Six standing stones had split corners in their crowns. They are closed now. The conifers also have fuller boughs without increasing their triangle count or file size.
