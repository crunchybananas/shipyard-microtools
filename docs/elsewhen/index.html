    Welcome to Elsewhen

    You can leave
    a little of yourself here.

    Touch the bells to hear them. Choose Leave a hand, then press and move across the instrument. Release to turn that gesture into a repeating memory.

    A still gesture leaves a still hand. A moving gesture leaves a path. Each memory repeats on the same eight-second clock. The part after you release holds your last position.

    Make a twin, play a memory backward, or move it one beat ahead. Up to six memories can keep you company.
