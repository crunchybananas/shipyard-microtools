Find your sound

  1. Press Play. Song travels through your arrangement. Loop stays in one scene.
  2. Launch a scene. It arrives at the next bar and keeps looping until you choose another.
  3. Move through the pad. Left to right opens the sound; upward adds space. Capture motion remembers one bar of movement.
  4. Make it yours. Edit the steps, select a voice to change notes and tone, or play along with A–K.
  5. Take it away. Export renders the score, mix, and captured motion. Live keyboard improvisation is for the moment; put those notes in the grid to include them in a WAV.

Use arrow keys on the performance pad. Tab reaches every control. Scenes use keys 1–4; Space starts and stops playback.
